# RR-07: Associate saved contacts with job opportunities

Status: open local draft; not a published GitHub issue.

Milestone: 4

Planned owner: @lakerss6 (A, you); reviewer: @joewahy (B). Recorded locally; not assigned on GitHub.

## Goal

Track a target job and the people associated with it.

## Dependencies

RR-06

## Starting points

New opportunities feature; shared contact IDs from RR-06

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Create/edit opportunities with company, role, optional job URL, application status, and date.
- [ ] Associate multiple saved contacts without duplicating their records.
- [ ] Keep application status distinct from contact or message status.
- [ ] Validate URLs and dates; deleting an opportunity preserves saved contacts.
- [ ] Persist through the existing demo-storage boundary.

## Guided first steps for beginners

1. Sketch an opportunity and its relationship to saved contact IDs.
2. Build a create/edit form with just company and role first.
3. Add optional fields, relationships, and persistence one small step at a time.

Work on one step at a time. Ask for explanations or hints when stuck; the debugging exercise is a learning prompt, not a deliberately planted bug.

## Learning objective

Relationships, form validation, and domain modeling.

## Debugging exercise

Associate the same contact with two opportunities and remove one; inspect the surviving relationship.

## Verification and handoff

Run `npm run typecheck` and `npm run build`. Record relevant manual steps and any focused tests. Have the reviewer reproduce one edge case. Explain one implementation decision in your own words.

## Out of scope

Job-board scraping or resume attachments.
